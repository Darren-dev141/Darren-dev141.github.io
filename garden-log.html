The Garden Log

A digital garden is not a blog and not a showcase. It's a collection of notes that stays open — planted early, tended in public, reorganized as the thinking changes. This one adds a rule most gardens don't have: maturity is evidence.

The stages

  • 🌱 Seedling — one source, untested, or an open question. Planted because it's worth watching, labeled because it isn't proven.
  • 🌿 Budding — tested once, or one real result on the record. It survived contact with reality a single time.
  • 🌳 Evergreen — survived falsification or replication: pre-registered predictions that held, controls that pinned the noise floor, or a machine-checked proof.

The tending rule

A plant's stage changes only when its evidence does — never because it got older, prettier, or more popular.
A claim rides in this garden only as long as its evidence does. When the evidence dies, the plant is dug up — removed or demoted — and the digging is written down here, with the reason.
Numbers carry their denominators. "Unverified" is a label, not an embarrassment.

That rule was not invented for this page. It's the rule the work itself produced, on Oct 22, 2026, when a day of falsification checks found dead job postings presented as verified, a decision engine repeating calls its operator had already rejected, and a router reporting itself "up" while its leader lane served zero calls. The garden is that lesson, given a permanent address.
