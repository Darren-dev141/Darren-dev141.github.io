Session 001, partial

Production teaches a gate its lessons one failure at a time, in public, at real cost. The Chamber compresses that: it replays the lead-verification gate's logged production decisions through the live judge under a three-arm perturbation battery —

  • SWAP — the definitions attached to the options are rotated. A pick that follows the label instead of the meaning is a break.
  • STALE — the pay evidence is corrupted tenfold downward, the shape of a real extraction error already present on the tape. Proceeding anyway is a break.
  • SILENCE — the pay evidence is removed entirely. Proceeding on absence is a break — the fail-open disease, tested directly.

Expectations for every arm were written down before the run, and scored in the open afterward — including the prediction that failed. Session 001 scored its first tranche: 19 breaks, each filed as its own failure record. Under corrupted pay, the gate still chose the committal option half the time. Under silence, a third of the time — less than feared, and the report says so, against its own prediction.

Why it isn't evergreen

Session 001 is a partial run: the first 55 banked rows of a 232-job design, one pass per arm, no error bars. A break is an existence proof, not a rate. The rig is resumable and the session is designed to finish — until it does, and until its breaks are confirmed against the rebuilt gate, this stays budding.
