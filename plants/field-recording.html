The premise of the whole garden, in one artifact. On Oct 22, 2026, the systems in Bed 1 spent the day being falsified — a job feed audited against employers' own lists, a decision ledger checked against the inbox, a router's receipts read against its health check. Field Recording №1 is that day, kept: 50 verbatim excerpts from the day's machine logs, in six movements, cuts marked […], every excerpt carrying its source beneath it.

The rule it demonstrates is the garden's rule: summaries never replace originals. Any claim anywhere in this garden traces back to tape like this. When two excerpts were found altered in assembly, they were rebuilt from the logs and re-verified — the recording keeps its own corrections, too.

Why it isn't evergreen

It's one day, verified against its own logs — a single, well-kept record, not a replicated finding. Evergreen here waits for №2: a second day recorded the same way, by the same standard, without knowing in advance what it will contain.
