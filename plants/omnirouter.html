Switchboard / Omnirouter

A router that receipts every call — so "the system is up" has to mean something.

Every AI call in this stack passes through one local router. For each call the router writes a receipt: which model was requested, which model actually served, what fell back, what errored. Three API dialects come in; a local embeddings lane runs on the same box; a lane watcher checks health per lane, from the receipts — never from a process being merely alive.

That discipline earned its keep on Oct 22. The router's health check said "up" all day. The receipts said something else: of the day's real calls, the leader lane served zero — it had been capped for two days while the process happily reported healthy. Health is now reported per lane, from served counts. A system that can't tell you it's degraded is worse than a degraded system.

Why it isn't evergreen

The accounting works; the fuel is the constraint. Paid lanes sit capped or unconfigured, and the honest status line reads "degraded — no leader" until that changes. This plant claims the receipts, not the fleet. It stays budding until the routing it advertises is the routing it can prove, over time.
