Digital
Garden

Notes, grown in public — organized by connection, not by date.

Darren Foote · data analyst · builder of decision systems

How this garden works: every plant is a note at a declared maturity stage, and the stage is set by its evidence — not its age, not its polish. A plant moves up only when its evidence does. When a claim's evidence dies, the plant gets dug up, and the digging is recorded in the garden log. Most of this garden is young. It says so on every label.

🌱Seedling

One source, untested — or an open question. Interesting enough to plant, not yet strong enough to lean on.

🌿Budding

Tested once, or one real result on the record. It survived contact with reality a single time.

🌳Evergreen

Survived falsification or replication — pre-registered predictions, controls, or a proof. The load-bearing plants.
